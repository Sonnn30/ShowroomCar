About us

Welcome to DiamondCUt Motors

DiamondCut Motors is a prestigious car reseller, dedicated to providing luxurious vehicles throughout Indonesia. Founded in 1998, we have been at the forefront of the luxury automobile market for over two decades, offering an exceptional range of renowned brands such as Porsche, Mercedes-Benz, Bugatti, and many others. Our esteemed clientele includes a number of business owners and successful individuals who are drawn to our cutting-edge service, timely delivery, and uncompromised vehicle safety.

Our History

DiamondCut Motors was established in 1998 with a vision to redefine luxury car reselling in Indonesia. Since our inception, we have experienced exponential growth, establishing ourselves as a trusted name in the industry. Our journey from a modest beginning to a market leader is a testament to our dedication, expertise, and the trust placed in us by our valued customers.
